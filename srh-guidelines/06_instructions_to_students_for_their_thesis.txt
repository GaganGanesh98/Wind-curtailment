===================================================
In order to be successful in your thesis you need to do the following:
===================================================

A. To find research papers please use the following sources: IEEE, ACM, ScienceDirect, Scopus, WebOfScience, Elsevier, Springer, etc. Please do NOT use anything from ResearchGate as reference directly in your thesis, rather you should further investigae where the pper was published!

B. How to read papers? - read the title first, then the abstract, the conclusion and most importantly the future research section!!! If you like the paper, keep it and log it in the table as described in point D below.

C. How many papers to read? - initially start with 5-10-15 papers you can find from the sources in A. The total sources for your thesis should be at least 25-30-40-50 at the end. You will increase them when you start working. You will reference each one in your thesis.

D. For all the research papers you found interesting in point B, prepare an excel table where on each row you describe each paper and the columns should contain the following: 1. number, 2. title, 3. source, 4. year published, 5. 3-5 sentences of your personal comments for the paper, 6. methodology used, 7. databases and URL sources if needed, 8. Miscellaneous.

E. At the end of your work, we will try to submit a paper to be published with your research. In order to find where to publish, here is a good source to find the best journal based on your topic: https://journalfinder.elsevier.com/ https://www.scimagojr.com/journalrank.php Just substitute the title and abstract of your future paper or from one that you really like and the results will show you the best match of where to publish. Then, look into 3 of them that have a higher rate of acceptance. See the requirements to submit a paper in each one. Look into the deadlines and the cost as well.

F. Here is a rough structure of the paper you will write as a result of your research and your work:
	1. Title
	2. Participants: Last and First names, institution / affiliation, email for each author
	3. Abstract: make it interesting and appealing to the reader. In a few sentences describe the uniqueness of your work with the top results you achieved
	4. Keywords: the most relevant terms related to the specific topic
	5. Introduction: here is the literature review with most of the references you will add at the end
	6. Other Sections and subsections pertaining to your methodology and unique contribution (different for everyone) - architecture, model, methods, formulas
	7. Results: graphs, tables, and discussion
	8. Conclusion: in 8-10 sentences describe the results of your contribution
	9. References - prepared usually in APA format !!! (Please see some YouTube videos of how this can be organized by you!)

G. Your thesis will be based on your research paper (if publication was accepted!), so the paper is going to be used as a background of your main thesis work. The paper should be between 8-12 pages long. The thesis should have a minimum of 20,000 words, including tables, figures, formulas, pivotal code snippets, references, etc.
