01_motivation_event_day

Motivation. 2026-04-05 (Easter Sunday) is the largest-error negative-price event-day of the forecast-bias check, not a typical day: it carries 24.8% of all negative-quarter-hour forecast error. The forecast-bias check covers 2025-10-01 to 2026-10-28, all of Germany including TenneT, 2,059 negative-price quarter-hours on 99 event days; that is not Experiment 3's window (2025-10-01 to 2026-09-30, three areas). Plotted here: onshore wind, 50Hertz + Amprion + TransnetBW (TenneT excluded): TSO day-ahead forecast F, actual output G and the quality-assured projection H (Online-Hochrechnung), with the day-ahead price on the right axis. Shaded: negative-price quarter-hours.

Source: Day chosen as rank 1 in archive/results/02_worst_days_de.csv (columns rank_by_total_error, share_of_total_negative_qh_error; ranked on DE total wind). Series from src/method/available_power.load_frames('quality-assured'): da_onshore_mw, act_onshore_mw, hr_mw, rd_mw, price_eur_mwh. Inputs data/processed/panel_qh.parquet and data/raw/netztransparenz/hochrechnung_wind_onshore_qualitaetsgesichert/ are not in git.
