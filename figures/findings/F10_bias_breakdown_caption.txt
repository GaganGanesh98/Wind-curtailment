F10_bias_breakdown

Phase V2: the bias-robust full-window interval with the projection H, main setting, for the four functionals with a reading in the conclusions table, against the bias budget B (the hull over every constant multiplicative bias |b| ≤ B on a 0.01 grid). Dashed line: the bound the reading needs (λ₁: width below 0.25, shown as the interval itself). Vertical line: the breakdown B, the smallest B at which the reading no longer holds. Dotted line, for scale only: |−0.0472|, the projection's measured bias on price ≥ 0 quarter-hours; its bias during negative prices is not measured.

Source: artifacts/V/v2_robust_intervals.csv (columns lam, B, lo, hi, width) and artifacts/V/v2_breakdown.csv (column breakdown_B); scale line from artifacts/Q0/q0_anchors.csv, column bias (anchor H, DE excl. TenneT, price >= 0).
