F4_coverage_never_fails

Coverage of the known truth by the identified sets in every synthetic setting that has a truth and an unbiased potential: P3 Arms O and D, every Q1 noise level, every Q6b setting. Dashed line: the pre-registered 95% target (binding for P3 Arm O). This holds for unbiased potentials only: with a biased potential coverage falls below 95% at every tested bias (Q2, docs/Q2_results.md); Phase V's bias-robust bounds address that (docs/V_results.md).

Source: artifacts/P3/p3_synthetic_arm_o.csv and p3_synthetic_arm_d.csv, column cov (via src/identification/summarise_p3.load); artifacts/Q1/q1_summary_by_level.csv, column level_coverage; artifacts/Q6/q6b_summary.csv, rows table = levels, column coverage.
