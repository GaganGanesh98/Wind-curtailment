Experiment 1: median width of the identified set (line) and interquartile range (band) across the 54 synthetic cells, against i.i.d. noise σ in the potential, on the tested grid only. Left: fleet functionals; right: cohort functionals. Log scale. Vertical lines: the statutory projection H (σ is a lower bound on its error; the operational series' value; the quality-assured series gives σ 0.0610 and bias −0.0326 (docs/03)) and the TSO forecast. Dashed horizontal line: 0.25, the informative limit for λ₁–λ₄ and λ₇ (λ₆'s is 0.5). λ₅ is omitted: it is an energy in GWh, not a share of potential.
